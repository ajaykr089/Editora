Headless collapsible, accordion, tooltip, hover card, toggle group and toolbar. `@editora/ui-core/headless` adds `createCollapsible`, `createAccordion`, `createTooltip`, `createHoverCard`, `createToggleGroup` and `createToolbar`, and `@editora/ui-react` adds the matching `useHeadless*` hooks: WAI-ARIA roles, keyboard handling and `data-state` on your own markup.
