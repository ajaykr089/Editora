`FiltersBar` has a date range: `dateRange` / `onDateRangeChange` add a date-range picker, and `dateRangePresets` one-click ranges beside it (the active one shows as pressed). `commonDateRangePresets` provides last 7 and 30 days, this and last month, and this year in the user's local time.
