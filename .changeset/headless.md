Headless behaviour, for your own markup and styles: `createDialog`, `createPopover`, `createMenu`, `createTabs` and `createListbox` (`@editora/ui-core/headless`) and the React hooks `useHeadlessDialog`, `useHeadlessPopover`, `useHeadlessMenu`, `useHeadlessTabs` and `useHeadlessListbox`. Each sets the WAI-ARIA roles and states and handles keyboard, focus (trap and return), dismissal and positioning, with `data-state` for styling, built on the same primitives as the styled components. See the "Headless hooks" docs page.
