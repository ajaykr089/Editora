Headless checkbox, switch, radio group and slider. `@editora/ui-core/headless` adds `createCheckbox`, `createSwitch`, `createRadioGroup` and `createSlider` (one thumb or a range), and `@editora/ui-react` adds `useHeadlessCheckbox`, `useHeadlessSwitch`, `useHeadlessRadioGroup` and `useHeadlessSlider`. With a `name` they take part in native forms: their values go into FormData, `required` works, and a form reset restores them.
