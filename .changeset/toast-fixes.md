Fix several toast bugs. `closable: true` was ignored (only its alias `closeButton` worked), because the config's `closeButton: false` was merged in first and then read ahead of it; an explicit `closable: false` also failed to override a configured `closeButton: true`, and `update()` now adds or removes the close button. `configure({ maxVisible })` never changed the limit (the previously stored `visibleToasts` alias always won). `html: true` rendered the literal markup instead of HTML, because its "sanitizer" escaped everything; it now renders a safe subset (inline formatting, lists and http(s)/mailto/tel/relative links) built from an allowlist, and removes everything else. A toast could stay on "loading" forever when its promise settled before the toast had been registered, and `update()`/`dismiss()` called in the same tick as `show()` were silently dropped; they now wait for the toast. The close button was invisible on touch devices (it only appeared on hover); it is now always visible there. A timer that removed the screen-reader announcement threw if the page body had already been replaced. Documents `html: true` and persistent toasts in the README, and adds the package's first test suite.
