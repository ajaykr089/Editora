New package: read and write Word .docx files with no dependencies, through the same document tree as @editora/rich-text-convert. Real headings, nested numbered lists, links, images, tables with merged cells, footnotes and tracked changes, structure inferred for files that have none, and warnings for anything that cannot be carried over. Includes an `editora-docx` CLI.
