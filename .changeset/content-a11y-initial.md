'@editora/content-a11y': minor

New package: accessibility checks for authored content (alt text, headings, link text, lists, tables, colour contrast and more, 25 rules mapped to WCAG 2.2) with fixes marked safe-to-apply or needs-a-person. Runs headless (`checkContent`, `fixContent`), as editor markers (`attachContentChecker`, Editora plugin with undoable fixes) and in CI (`content-a11y` CLI).
